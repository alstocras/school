The main idea behind my artwork is how I prioritize certain parts of me, compared to others, by making my head and eyes in larger hexagons than my mouth, and this shows how those bits are more important to me. Presenting myself as a normal shoulder-portrait was too\dots normal. So I changed the presentation style: I am now 6 disjointed regular hexagons. I was influenced by the symmetry of regular hexagons and the cleanliness of minimalist duotone art. I chose the colours simply because I thought they looked nice, but also because of the sharp contrast between orange and blue, with the violet being a kind of equalizer. I wanted to show how different I am, and the diversity of my face. I also picked oil pastels for their niceness. I faced challenges of trying not to make my art look tacky with more than two colours, but I was able to use pleasing colours to fix this. When a viewer sees my artwork, I want them to notice the choice of colours, and the deliberate use of minimal colour. I learnt that I am far better at minimalist art than hyper-detailed realistic art, and I am happy about it.
